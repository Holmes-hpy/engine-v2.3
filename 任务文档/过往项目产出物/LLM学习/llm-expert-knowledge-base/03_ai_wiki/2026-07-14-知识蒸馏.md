  - Terminal embeddings have been particularly fruitful for constructing $k$-means and $k$-median coresets, where the objective is to find a typically weighted subset $Ω$ of $P$ such that for any candidate solution, the cost of the clustering objective on $Ω$ approximates the cost of the clustering objective on $P$ up to small distortion
